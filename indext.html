Welcome to TS Babylonia -
a space for reharmonizing scatteredness
by overstanding our programming
through waves of sound and jibberish

From the bottom of the compost arises TS Babylonia -
the troubadour who travels past communities
to pop bubbles and expand horizons
passing on information.
The message: we all need to look within
in order to overstand
and regain power
over where our own energy travels

Through song I channel new ways for peace :
always recalculating
put every-one in the mix
to give space for essence

My safe space is a recording studio <3
